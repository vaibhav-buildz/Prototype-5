Need Answers? We Got You.

The most common questions about the Blazion infrastructure layer and our roadmap.

What does "AI-Native" actually mean?
Unlike tools that "add" AI as a wrapper, Blazion is built with intelligence at the kernel level. Every API, form validation, and database query is designed to be understood and optimized by agents in real-time.
Is my data secure in the Blazion ecosystem?
Everything is DPDP-compliant and encrypted at rest with industry-standard 256-bit AES. We offer Indian-region hosting by default for all local fintech compliance.
How does Form-to-WhatsApp work?
Blazion Form integrates directly with the WhatsApp Business API, allowing you to trigger flows and receive responses directly in chat without building a custom webhook handler.
